Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Who operates POG Studio

POG Studio is a personal tool operated by Garadron (contact: emmanuel.surace@gmail.com). It runs on the operator's own computer and is used only by the operator, with the operator's own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

What data it accesses

  • TikTok: basic account information (account identifier, display name and avatar) through the user.info.basic permission, and permission to upload videos to the account's drafts through the video.upload permission.
  • Instagram: basic information about the operator's professional account and permission to publish content to it.
  • Videos: only clips cut from the operator's own stream recordings.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the operator's clips to the operator's own accounts, after the operator has reviewed and approved them. POG Studio does not access other users' data.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens and clips are stored only on the operator's computer. POG Studio has no server and no database of its own: data is sent only to the TikTok and Instagram APIs when a clip is uploaded.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. POG Studio uses no cookies and no tracking.

Retention and deletion

Access tokens are kept until they expire or are revoked. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok or Instagram account settings; the operator can delete the locally stored tokens and clips at any time.

Children

POG Studio is not directed at children and is used only by its adult operator.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
