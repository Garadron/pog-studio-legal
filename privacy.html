Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Who operates POG Studio

POG Studio is a personal tool operated by Garadron (contact: emmanuel.surace@gmail.com). It runs on the operator's own computer and is used only by the operator, with the operator's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

YouTube API Services

POG Studio uses YouTube API Services. The YouTube data it handles is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy, and using POG Studio means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube, after the operator signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0):
    • with the youtube.upload permission, it uploads the clips the operator approves to the operator's own channel as Shorts (videos.insert);
    • with the youtube.readonly permission, it reads the statistics (views, likes, comments), the publication date, the title and the description of the operator's own Shorts of these clips (videos.list).
    It does not read or change any other channel, video, comment or account.
  • TikTok: basic account information (account identifier, display name and avatar) through the user.info.basic permission, permission to upload videos to the account's drafts through the video.upload permission, and the list and statistics of the operator's own public videos through the video.list permission.
  • Instagram: basic information about the operator's professional account, permission to publish content to it, and the statistics of the operator's own Reels.
  • Videos: only clips cut from the operator's own stream recordings.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the operator's clips to the operator's own accounts, after the operator has reviewed and approved them, and to show the operator how those clips are doing. The statistics are shown only to the operator, in the operator's private chat with POG Studio. POG Studio does not access other users' data.

YouTube statistics are shown exactly as YouTube returns them, labeled as YouTube's, and kept apart from the other platforms' numbers: POG Studio never adds them to other numbers or calculates new metrics from them. When the operator has rewritten a Short's title or description on YouTube, the rewritten text is kept as an example of the operator's writing style for the titles POG Studio suggests next; this happens only on the operator's computer, with no third-party service.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens, clips and statistics are stored only on the operator's computer. POG Studio has no server and no database of its own: data is exchanged only with the YouTube, TikTok and Instagram APIs. While POG Studio runs, the statistics are refreshed from the APIs every 6 hours.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. POG Studio uses no cookies, no similar technology and no tracking, and it does not allow third parties to serve content or advertisements.

Retention, deletion and revoking access

Access tokens are kept until they expire or are revoked; the operator can delete the locally stored tokens, clips and statistics at any time. Data obtained from YouTube (statistics, titles and descriptions) is read again every 6 hours while POG Studio runs, and is deleted if it has not been read again within 30 days. When access to YouTube is revoked, POG Studio deletes all the data it obtained from YouTube at its next refresh (every 6 hours while it runs, and right after it starts). Access to the YouTube account can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Access to TikTok and Instagram can be revoked from those accounts' settings.

Children

POG Studio is not directed at children and is used only by its adult operator.
