POG Studio

A personal tool by Garadron

POG Studio runs on its operator's own computer. After a live stream it cuts highlight clips from the operator's own recordings, lets the operator review them, and sends the approved ones to the operator's own social accounts: as drafts on TikTok, and as Reels on Instagram.

It is not a public service: nobody else can sign in to it or use it.
